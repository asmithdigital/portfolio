Work/Workshop Facilitation

Facilitating discovery — getting the right people in the room to solve the right problem.

Workshops and design sprints are how a cross-functional team moves from individual opinions to a shared, evidence-based decision. Running them well means the right structure, strict timeboxing, and leaving with something the team can act on.

Two design sprints facilitated using Google Ventures methodology, alongside ongoing ideation workshops, requirements gathering sessions, and cross-functional discovery work.

What is a Design Sprint?

A structured, time-boxed process for moving from problem to tested solution.

The Google Ventures Design Sprint is a framework for answering critical business questions through rapid design and user testing. It brings together a cross-functional group including product, engineering, design, and stakeholders, and moves them through problem definition, ideation, prototyping, and testing in a compressed timeframe.

The value is not the speed. It is the discipline. By timeboxing every activity and running individual work in parallel rather than group discussion, the sprint surfaces what the team actually knows, what it assumes, and where the evidence points. A nominated Decider makes the final call. The Facilitator keeps the process honest.

Sprint 1 — QTB Payments

RAA Quote to Buy — Payments Design Sprint

RAA's online insurance purchase platform had a conversion problem at the payment stage. The business assumed the fix was adding more payment methods. Before committing delivery capacity to that assumption, a design sprint was run to find out whether it was actually true.

The sprint synthesised five years of existing research including usability studies, GA4 analytics, journey maps, and third-party UX diagnostics into a single structured reference before any ideation began. Two senior leadership sessions were built in — the first to align on the right problems, the second to review prototypes and determine what would proceed to delivery.

The synthesis revealed the real blocker: around 38 to 40 percent of quotes were ending as deferred because RAA could not verify the member's identity to proceed. The problem was member matching, not payment method. That finding redirected the product roadmap. Authenticated quote-to-buy is now live for motor insurance.

Sprint 2 — Travel Discovery

RAA Travel — Discovery Design Sprint

A design sprint run as part of the discovery program for the RAA Travel platform — bringing together product managers, travel agents, marketing, and engineering to map the end-to-end customer journey and identify the highest-impact opportunities for the redesigned booking experience.

The sprint began with in-person customer interviews conducted in RAA stores alongside the product manager. The team then mapped the full customer journey on a whiteboard from awareness through booking and post-travel, with back-stage constraints from travel operations visible alongside the front-stage customer experience. The sprint outputs shaped the design direction for the Travel platform rebuild that followed.
