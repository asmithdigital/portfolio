Work/Working with UI design teams

Working with UI design teams across digital products.

Since then, as senior UX lead, I have worked with the UI design leads in my team and in other teams, and with external vendors, within Agile squads and the product trio. Starting in front-end development means working with engineers is a peer conversation, not a handover.

Rapid, low-fidelity prototypes that work within the design system, tested with users and iterated for 800,000+ RAA members.

Prototypes and Iteration

Low-fidelity prototypes that work within the design system.

I use my knowledge of UI fundamentals — typography, colour, layout, hierarchy, components, and accessibility — along with common interface patterns and users' mental models to build rapid, low-fidelity prototypes, layouts, and screens. They work within the design system and match what people expect, so ideas can be put in front of users quickly.

Each round is tested, and the insights shape the next iteration with the UI design leads and the rest of the squad. The Figma files below show the breadth of product areas this covers — identity management and authentication, payments and renewals, claims submission, and the My Account portal.
